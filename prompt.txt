Write one original one-line joke about crypto and trading life, under 200 characters, for the "Laugh Track: The Crypto Trading Joke Contest" on make.fun.

The joke must:
- Be a single line about crypto trading life, with a clear setup and a surprise turn.
- Land a real laugh with a payoff that rewards the setup, punchline at the end.
- Feel true to trading life, with a recognisable detail (the chart, the bag, the dip, the group chat).
- Be tight: every word earns its place, no throat clearing, no filler.
- Hold up on a second read — a line a trader would repeat to a friend.

Avoid generic crypto jokes, clichés without a twist, and anything that needs more than one line to land. Aim for roughly 140–180 characters so the line stays punchy and well under the 200-character cap.
